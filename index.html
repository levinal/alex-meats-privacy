ALEX MEATS – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

ALEX MEATS uses WhatsApp to communicate with customers about products, orders, delivery, and customer support. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be received through those conversations and how it is used.

Information We May Receive

When you contact ALEX MEATS through WhatsApp, we may receive:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number.
  • Your WhatsApp profile name, when available.
  • Messages you send to ALEX MEATS.
  • Information you voluntarily provide for an order, such as order and delivery details.

Please provide only the information needed for your inquiry or order.

How We Use Information

We use information received through WhatsApp to:

  • Respond to customer messages.
  • Provide product and price information.
  • Process and manage orders.
  • Coordinate delivery.
  • Provide customer support.

We use this information only as necessary to communicate with you and provide the requested service.

Sale of Personal Information

ALEX MEATS does not sell customers' personal information.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of information you have provided to ALEX MEATS by contacting ALEX MEATS through WhatsApp. Please state that you are requesting deletion of your information so the request can be handled.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact ALEX MEATS through WhatsApp.
